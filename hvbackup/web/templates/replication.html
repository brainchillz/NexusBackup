A replication keeps a powered-off standby copy of each VM on another connection (another vCenter, a standalone host, a Proxmox cluster), refreshed after every backup or on a schedule. Failover powers the copy on.

Replicas One standby VM per source VM and target. A copy is "behind" until the next sync carries it to the newest restore point.
